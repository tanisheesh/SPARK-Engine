==================================
NOTES
==================================

- All plans include local DuckDB execution — data never leaves your machine.
- "Managed keys" means SPARK provides its own Groq + Deepgram API keys.
  FREE users must supply their own (BYOK — Bring Your Own Keys).
- Yearly billing saves ~17% compared to monthly.
- STORM price covers a full 5-seat team (not per seat).
- THUNDER pricing is per seat per year — contact sales for a custom quote.
- Tiers reset query counts on the 1st of each calendar month (UTC).
